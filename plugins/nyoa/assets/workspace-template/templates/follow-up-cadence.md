# Follow-up Cadences

When and how to follow up by lead source / stage.

## Hot lead (just talked to)
- Day 0: text recap
- Day 1: email with curated listings
- Day 3: phone check-in
- Day 7: email with market update

## Warm lead (initial contact, no clear timeline)
- Day 0: thank-you
- Day 7: useful resource
- Day 21: market update
- Day 60: check-in

## Past client (post-close)
- Day 14: review request
- Month 3: check-in / market value update
- Month 12: anniversary message + market value update
- Annually: holiday note
