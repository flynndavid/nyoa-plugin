# Seller Weekly Update

The Friday recap. Even if there's nothing to report, the agent should send one. Sellers who hear from their agent every Friday don't fire their agent.

**Required inputs:** Seller first name, address, list price, days on market this week, showings count, online listing views, saves/shares, any feedback themes, comp activity in the neighborhood, recommendation (hold / consider price / aggressive change).

**Channels:** Email only (this is a record-of-communication asset).

---

## Structure

### Subject
```
Friday update on {{address}} — {{headline_in_5_words}}
```

Example: "Friday update on 123 Maple — 8 showings, no offer yet"

### Opening (1 sentence)
What's the headline reality of this week, plain.

### Activity block
- Showings this week: {{n}}
- Online listing views: {{n}} (week-over-week: {{up/down}})
- Saves: {{n}}
- Open house attendance (if applicable): {{n}}

### Feedback themes (if any)
2–3 bullets of what showing agents are saying.

### Comp activity
- New listings in your range: {{addresses + prices}}
- Price changes / sales: {{addresses + outcomes}}

### Recommendation
One paragraph. Hold? Consider a small price test? Bigger move? Why.

### Close
What the agent will do next week. One ask if applicable.

---

## Example

```
Subject: Friday update on 123 Maple — 8 showings, no offer yet

Hey Mike,

Steady week, no offer yet, but the activity tells me we're not far off. Recap below.

Activity this week:
· 8 showings (4 last weekend, 4 spread mid-week)
· 1,140 listing views on Zillow (+18% w/w)
· 22 saves, 9 shares
· Open house Sunday: 11 groups through

Feedback themes from showing agents:
· Kitchen + finishes: consistently positive
· Backyard: some flagged the lack of fence (3 separate buyers mentioned)
· Price: two showing agents said "right at the top of comparable comps"

Comp activity in the neighborhood this week:
· 312 Maple closed at $612K (was $625K, 22 DOM)
· 408 Locust hit MLS at $629K — newer comp than ours

Recommendation:
Right now I'd hold the price one more week. The Sunday open hit hard and we have two repeat-buyer agents who said they're bringing clients next weekend. If we get to next Friday with no offer, the 312 Maple comp gives us a defensible reason to move to $619K, and I think that's where this clears.

I'll send a separate note Tuesday if anything from the weekend changes the picture.

— Ann-Riley
```

## Hard rules

- One subject line that headlines the truth of the week, not "weekly update" generic
- No "we're still optimistic" filler when activity is bad. Sellers can read activity numbers; lying with tone breaks trust.
- Always end with: what happens next.
