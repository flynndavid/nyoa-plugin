# Counter-Offer Draft

The agent-to-agent message that accompanies a counter-offer being sent through the contract software. Short, professional, gives the buyer's agent the rationale so they can sell the counter to their buyer.

**Required inputs:** Buyer's agent first name, original offer amount, counter amount, counter-changed terms (earnest, closing date, contingencies, inclusions), key reason for the counter.

**Channels:** Email primarily (and the contract software). Some agents text — see SMS variant.

---

## Email variant

```
Subject: Counter on {{address}} — {{counter_amount}}

Hey {{buyer_agent_first}},

Sending the counter through {{contract_platform}} now. Quick recap so you can walk your client through it:

We're at {{counter_amount}} ({{change_from_original}} change), with:
· Earnest: {{earnest_amount}}
· Closing: {{closing_date}}
· {{any other changed terms}}

Why we're here:
{{1–2 sentences. Comp evidence + market evidence. Avoid emotional language. Lead with facts your buyer's agent can use.}}

The seller is real and motivated — they want this to work, but {{counter_amount}} is the number that matches what they're seeing in the comps right now. Happy to talk it through if helpful.

— {{agent_first}}
```

---

## SMS variant (only if you and the buyer's agent already text)

```
Counter on {{address}} going through {{contract_platform}} — {{counter_amount}}, {{key_changed_term}}. Comps from this week support it. Holler if you want to talk it through. — {{agent_first}}
```

---

## Example

```
Subject: Counter on 123 Maple — $619K

Hey Sarah,

Sending the counter through Dotloop now. Quick recap for your client:

We're at $619K (up $14K from the offer), with:
· Earnest: $10,000 (was $5K)
· Closing: June 15 — accepted as-is
· Chandelier stays excluded
· All other terms accepted

Why we're here:
312 Maple closed last week at $612K, and that house had no walk-up attic. The Wolf range and the original 1924 detail aren't showing up in the comps the appraiser will pull. $619K matches the price-per-foot of the closer comp set.

Mike is real and motivated — the open this Sunday had two repeat agents asking about it, so we have some movement on our side. Want to make this work; just need to hit a number that holds at appraisal.

Happy to talk it through if that helps you walk your buyer through it.

— Ann-Riley
```

## Hard rules

- Never write the counter terms in tone; just the facts.
- Lead the rationale with comp / market evidence the other agent can use, not "the seller really feels".
- Don't undersell the seller's leverage in the body; one true line about momentum is fine, three is begging.
- Sign agent-to-agent first name only.
