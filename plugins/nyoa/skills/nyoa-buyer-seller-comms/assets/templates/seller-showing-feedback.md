# Seller Showing Feedback Summary

Sent ad hoc when there's been a meaningful chunk of showings (e.g., the weekend) and patterns are forming. Often a follow-up to the weekly update — or replaces a weekly update if there's nothing else to say.

**Required inputs:** Seller first name, address, list price, n recent showings, raw feedback notes from each showing agent.

**Channels:** Email.

---

## Structure

### Subject
```
Feedback from this weekend's showings — {{address}}
```

### Opening (1 sentence)
What you're sending and how many showings it covers.

### Themes
2–4 bullets, ranked by frequency. Each bullet:
- Theme + how many agents/buyers raised it
- Direction (positive / mixed / concern)
- The actual phrasing where useful

### What this tells us
1 short paragraph synthesizing the themes into a read on where the listing stands.

### Recommendation
What you'd do (hold, adjust copy, adjust photos, adjust price, adjust staging).

---

## Example

```
Subject: Feedback from this weekend's showings — 123 Maple

Hey Mike,

Pulling the weekend together. 8 showings + open house. Feedback themes below.

Themes (in order of frequency):

· Kitchen + finishes — strongly positive across all 8 showings. Multiple "best kitchen we've seen in this range".
· Backyard fence — 4 of 8 specifically mentioned the lack of fence as a knock. Two had dogs.
· Primary bath — 3 of 8 said it felt small relative to the rest of the house.
· Price — 2 showing agents said "fair, right at top of comparable range". 1 said "they'll need to come down 15K to compete with 312 Maple".

What this tells us:
The kitchen is doing the work. The lack of fence is the consistent pain point — that's a $1,500–$3,000 fix that would close 4 of the next 8 showings' #1 complaint. The bath comment is real but not a deal-killer at this price. Price is right at the top, not over.

Recommendation:
1. Get a fence quote this week. If it's <$3K, install. If it's $3–6K, talk through whether to credit at close.
2. Don't move on price yet. Give it one more weekend with the fence either in or credited.
3. I'm going to add a line to the listing copy about the lot's natural privacy from the back hedge so the no-fence read is softened until we install.

— Ann-Riley
```

## Hard rules

- Quote agent feedback verbatim where useful — gives the seller proof, not just the agent's read.
- Rank themes by frequency, not by how flattering they are.
- If feedback is bad, lead with the bad. Don't bury it.
