# Channel Conventions

Per-channel rules for SMS, email, and voicemail script outputs across all `nyoa-buyer-seller-comms` templates.

---

## SMS

- ≤320 characters (2 standard SMS segments) for the full message
- No "Hi {first}" — too template-y. Lead with the substance.
- No emojis (default). One subtle one is OK if the agent's voice file uses them.
- No links wider than ~30 chars. Use a shortener if needed.
- Sign with first name or initials (e.g., "— AR") for any recipient who might not have the agent's number saved.
- Imperative or declarative, not "I just wanted to let you know that…"
- One ask, one action. Two if absolutely necessary.

**SMS example (price drop):**

> 312 Maple — the one you walked Tuesday — just dropped from $649K to $619K. If that changes the math, I have a Saturday slot. — AR

(146 chars.)

---

## Email

- Subject ≤55 chars when possible. Specific. Concrete. No emojis.
- Skip "Hi {first}, I hope this email finds you well." Just say it.
- ≤180 words for routine messages, ≤300 for offer summaries and weekly updates.
- Plain text or very light HTML. No fancy templates. No giant agent signature blocks.
- Sign with first name only.
- One clear ask + one clear path (reply, schedule link, document attached).

**Email subject examples:**

- "Quick note on 312 Maple — price moved" ✅
- "Friday update on your listing — three showings + a price comp" ✅
- "🔥 Hot Price Drop Alert ‼️ Don't Miss!" ❌

---

## Voicemail script

Designed to be left as a 25–40 second voicemail.

- ≤80 words spoken
- Includes natural pauses (write "[brief pause]" or "—" where it makes sense)
- Says the agent's name twice (start + end)
- Says the callback number once at the end, slowly written: "615 dot 555 dot 0123" (cues the agent to read it slowly)
- Substance first, pleasantries trimmed
- One reason to call back

**Voicemail example (offer):**

> Hi [name], it's Ann-Riley with SimpliHOM — [brief pause] — we got an offer on 312 Maple this morning. It's $605K with a 30-day close and a $5K earnest. There's one inspection contingency I want to walk you through before we respond. Give me a call when you have ten minutes — I'll be at 615-555-0123. Again, that's Ann-Riley, 615-555-0123. Thanks.

(72 words.)

---

## Cross-channel rules

- Never categorize the recipient by protected class. Don't write "for buyers like you" if "like you" implies family/age/religion/origin.
- "Primary bedroom" not "master bedroom".
- No "JUST LISTED!!!" / "PRICE DROP!" all-caps shouty energy in any channel.
- No "Hope all is well" template-speak.
- If you don't have the recipient's name, say so to the agent and ask, rather than guessing.
