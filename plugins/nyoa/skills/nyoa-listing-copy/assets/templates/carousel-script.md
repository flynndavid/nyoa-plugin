# Instagram Carousel Script — 7 Slides

A pre-launch carousel that delivers the listing's story across seven slides. Pairs with the photographer's deliverables; the agent (or their VA) drops the photos and copy into Canva.

**Required:** address, 5-8 standout features, 7 photo references (filename or 1-line description), beds/baths/sqft, price, open-house time if scheduled.

---

## Structure

The seven slides do three jobs in order:

1. **Slide 1 (cover)** — stop the scroll.
2. **Slides 2-6 (features)** — five rooms or features, one per slide.
3. **Slide 7 (CTA)** — get the action.

The carousel works on Instagram (where carousels get higher reach than single-image posts) and reformats easily to Stories, a Reel cover, or a printed handout.

## Slide-by-slide skeleton

### Slide 1 — Cover

- **Background:** Photo 1 (typically the strongest exterior or hero shot).
- **Headline (≤ 8 words):** the hook from the MLS remarks. Not "Just Listed". A real sentence that signals the story.
- **Subhead:** Address + neighborhood. Skip the city if the neighborhood is recognizable.

### Slides 2-6 — Features

One slide per feature. Each slide has:

- **Background:** the relevant photo.
- **Headline (≤ 8 words):** the feature, framed as a sentence not a label. "The kitchen that closes the deal" beats "Kitchen".
- **Supporting line (≤ 20 words):** what about the feature makes it the deal-closer. Concrete specifics, not adjectives.
- **Detail callout:** one fact in 2-4 words ("Renovated 2024", "Original 1924 floors", "0.28 acre lot"). Sits in a corner of the slide.

The five feature slides should hit a mix: typically two interior rooms (kitchen + primary), one secondary room or moment (living, hallway, attic), one exterior (yard, porch, view), one detail close-up (a finish, a fixture, a craftsmanship moment).

### Slide 7 — CTA

- **Headline (≤ 8 words):** "Want to see it?" / "Walk it Saturday?" / specific call.
- **Address** (full).
- **Price.**
- **Open house time** if there is one.
- **Action:** "DM me to schedule a private showing." / "Comment OPEN to get the link." / single specific verb.
- **Footer:** agent name + brokerage + license number (small, but required).

## Example (East Nashville, fictional)

### Slide 1 — Cover
- Background: front exterior, twilight
- Headline: "Eleven tours later, this is the one."
- Subhead: "312 Maple St · East Nashville"

### Slide 2 — Kitchen
- Background: kitchen wide
- Headline: "The kitchen that closes the deal."
- Supporting: "Quartz, six-burner Wolf, walk-in pantry. Designed for the cook who actually cooks."
- Detail: "Renovated 2024"

### Slide 3 — Living
- Background: living room wide
- Headline: "Where you actually live."
- Supporting: "Original 1924 white oak, refinished. Natural light from three exposures."
- Detail: "Original floors"

### Slide 4 — Primary
- Background: primary bedroom + en-suite
- Headline: "Quiet, private, not an afterthought."
- Supporting: "Renovated bath. Walk-in closet. Door closes."
- Detail: "Bath renovated 2023"

### Slide 5 — Backyard
- Background: backyard + porch
- Headline: "The reason offers come fast."
- Supporting: "Screened porch. Fenced yard. Mature trees that didn't get cut for a deck."
- Detail: "0.28 acre lot"

### Slide 6 — Detail
- Background: a close-up — the Wolf range, the original transom window, the porch railing
- Headline: "The details that hold up."
- Supporting: "Real materials, not cosmetic shortcuts."
- Detail: (skip — let the photo speak)

### Slide 7 — CTA
- Headline: "Want to see it?"
- Address: 312 Maple St, East Nashville
- Price: $625,000
- Open House: Sunday, 1–3pm
- Action: "DM me to schedule a private showing."
- Footer: Ann-Riley Caldwell · SimpliHOM · TN License #00000000

## Compliance check

- "Primary bedroom" not "master".
- No "perfect for families", "great schools", "diverse neighborhood", "exclusive". Speak to the property.
- Renovation claims must be confirmed (already in `property.md` or the agent's brief).
- The story must be true. If "eleven tours" wasn't real, write something else specific.
- License + brokerage on the CTA slide.

## Tips

- For a 10-slide version: add three more middle slides for additional rooms (typically guest bedroom, dining, a second feature close-up).
- For a Reels reformat: same seven beats, but the cover becomes the hook (0-3s), the five features become 3-15s of cuts, the CTA becomes 25-30s with on-screen text.
- Hashtags go on the parent post caption (not the slides). 12-15 tags, neighborhood + market + niche.
