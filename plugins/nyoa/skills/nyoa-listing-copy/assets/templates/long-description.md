# Long Description Template

**Length:** 300–500 words. Three or four short paragraphs.

## Structure

### Paragraph 1 — The walk-up scene
Open with what a buyer sees, hears, or feels arriving at the house. Image-forward, single concrete moment. Not "you'll fall in love"; instead "you'll notice the original front-door leaded glass before you knock".

### Paragraph 2 — The house itself
Specific rooms, specific finishes, specific dimensions. Tie features to use:
- "the third bedroom is large enough for a queen bed and a full desk" beats "spacious bedroom"
- "a six-burner Wolf range and quartz counters" beats "modern kitchen"
- "primary bedroom with a walk-in closet that holds a 60-inch dresser" beats "huge closet"

### Paragraph 3 — Lot, location, context
The yard, the lot, the immediate block, walkability, neighborhood. **Verifiable claims only.** Don't invent school districts. Don't claim "best block" without source.

### Paragraph 4 — The close
Who this home is right for + the action. Ends on a buyer feeling, not an exclamation point.

## Hard rules

- 300–500 words
- 0 clichés
- 0 Fair Housing red flags
- 0 unsourced structural claims
- 0 protected-class implications
- "Primary bedroom" not "master bedroom"
- Tie at least 3 features to a specific buyer use ("fits a queen + desk", "holds a 60-inch dresser", etc.)

## Variables

- {{walkup_scene}}
- {{interior_features}} (4–6 specific, named, dimensioned)
- {{lot_and_lifestyle}}
- {{buyer_target}} (first-time / move-up / luxury / investor / downsizer / land)
- {{close_action}} (open house / schedule / window of availability)

## Example skeleton

```
You'll notice [walkup_scene_specific_detail] before you reach the front door. [Continue scene 1–2 sentences.]

Inside, [interior_anchor_feature_1]. [Specific room w/ dimension or use case.] [Specific finish or material.] [Second anchor feature with use case.]

The lot [lot_specific_detail]. [Block / walk / neighborhood verifiable detail.] [One scene that ties location to lifestyle.]

For a [buyer_target] who [specific behavior — cooks, works from home, has a dog, restores old houses], this is the rare one. [close_action — open house time, scheduling note, or single call-to-action verb.]
```
