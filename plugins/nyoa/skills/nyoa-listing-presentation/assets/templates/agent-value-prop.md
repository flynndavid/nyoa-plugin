**5. [Specific Differentiator]** (optional)

## Example Reasons (customize to agent)

- **Local expertise:** "XX homes sold in [neighborhood] in the last 24 months — more than any other agent in the area."
- **Marketing advantage:** "Every listing gets a professional copy package, social campaign, and email blast on day one — not a template from the brokerage."
- **Track record:** "Average DOM of XX days vs. the market average of XX days."
- **Client proof:** "[Testimonial from proofs.md about seller experience]"
- **Specific skill:** "[Negotiation track record, specific credential, bilingual, relocation expertise, etc.]"

If `nyoa-context/proofs.md` has testimonials tagged as "seller" — use one as a pull-quote in this section.
