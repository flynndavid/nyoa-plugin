# Market Narrative

2–3 paragraphs interpreting the current market for this property's neighborhood and price tier. Structure:

**Paragraph 1 — Market snapshot:**
What's happening in this submarket right now. Reference specific comp data: average DOM, price trends (up/down/flat from the comp set), inventory levels if known. Use numbers, not adjectives. "Homes in this price tier are averaging 18 days on market" not "the market is hot."

**Paragraph 2 — Property positioning:**
How does this specific property compare to what's recently sold? Stronger or weaker than the comp average? What features differentiate it (positively or negatively)? Reference specific comps: "The Maple Drive sale at $612K had a similar floor plan but lacked the updated kitchen."

**Paragraph 3 (optional) — Context or risk:**
Any external factors the seller should know: seasonal trends, interest rate environment, new construction nearby, school year timing. Only include if relevant and verifiable. Skip this paragraph rather than padding with generic "market conditions can change" filler.

**Rules:**
- Every claim must be traceable to a comp or verifiable data point
- No "the market is strong/hot/cool" without a number backing it
- No predictions — frame as "based on recent activity" not "the market will"
- No neighborhood demographic claims (Fair Housing)
