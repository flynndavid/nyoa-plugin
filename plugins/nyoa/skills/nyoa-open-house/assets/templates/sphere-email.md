# Open House — Sphere Email

**Required:** address, date, time window, list price, agent first name + signature block.
**Length:** ≤ 200 words. One light CTA.
**Audience:** people who like the agent. Not buyers in market — the sphere. Tone is "drop by, no pressure", not "this is THE house".

---

## Structure

1. **Subject** — specific and uninstagram-y. "Sunday open house at 312 Maple" beats "Don't miss this stunning home!".
2. **Opening line** — a sentence the agent could plausibly say to a friend. Not "I hope this email finds you well".
3. **Two-line property summary** — beds/baths, neighborhood, list price, one signature feature.
4. **Logistics** — date, window, address. Parking note if relevant.
5. **Soft CTA** — "stop by, no need to RSVP" or "let me know if you want a private walk".
6. **Sign-off** — agent first name + signature block.

## Skeleton

```
Subject: {{specific_subject}}

Hey {{first_name}},

{{opening_line — sounds like a friend, not a flyer}}.

{{two_line_property_summary}}

{{date_full}}, {{time_window}}. {{address}}. {{parking_note_if_needed}}.

{{soft_cta}}.

{{agent_first}}
{{agent_signature_block}}
```

## Compliance check

- No demographic targeting in the audience pitch ("perfect for your kids" → not allowed).
- No "must see" / "won't last".
- License + brokerage in the signature.
- No promises about whether the buyer will love it. Keep claims to features.
