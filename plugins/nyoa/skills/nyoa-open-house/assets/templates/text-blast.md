# Open House — Text Blast

**Required:** date short, window, address short, list price.
**Length:** Single SMS — ≤ 160 chars. No emojis. No "Hi {first}".
**Audience:** active leads on a list the agent has actual permission to text.

---

## Structure

A text blast does one job: get the recipient to know the open house is happening at a specific time and place. That's it. No selling in 160 chars.

## Skeleton

```
{{date_short}} {{time_window_short}} — open house {{address_short}}. {{beds}}BD {{baths}}BA, listed {{price_short}}. Reply STOP to opt out.
```

## Example

> Sun 1–3 — open house 312 Maple, East Nashville. 3BD 2BA, listed $625K. Reply STOP to opt out.

**That's 96 characters.** Stay under 160 even after personalization tokens expand.

## Compliance check

- Recipient must have prior consent. The agent confirms the list before sending.
- Opt-out language is required (TCPA).
- No protected-class language.
- No "must see" / "won't last".
- Send during reasonable hours for the recipient's time zone (TCPA recommends 8am–9pm local).
