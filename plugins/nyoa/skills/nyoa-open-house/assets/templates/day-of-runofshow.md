# Open House — Day-of Run-of-Show

A checklist, not a script. The agent walks through it once an hour before doors and once at the end.

---

## T-minus-90 (90 minutes before doors)

- Arrive at the property.
- Confirm sellers and pets are out. Re-confirm pet location if any animal is staying on-site.
- Lights on everywhere (including closets).
- Counters clear, beds made, toilet seats down, towels straight.
- Coffee / refreshments staged (if applicable).
- Sign-in clipboard or tablet at the door, with the sign-in card copy visible.
- Yard signs out: directional signs at the nearest two intersections + the property sign visible from the street.
- Door open and lockbox closed.

## T-minus-30

- Post the Stories T-minus-4h frame if you haven't.
- One slow walk of the property — front door, kitchen, primary bedroom, backyard. Spot-check for anything off.
- Cars off the driveway (yours and any helpers'). Driveway empty reads "the house is the focus".
- Phone on silent. Voice memo or notes app open for verbal feedback later.

## T-0 (doors open)

- Greet at the door. Hand the sign-in card and a one-page flyer (if you have one).
- Live Stories frame within the first 15 minutes.
- Don't follow attendees room-to-room unless they invite it. Be available at the kitchen island or front porch.

## During (T+0 → T+end)

- Note what each visitor lingers on. The kitchen? The backyard? The attic? File that observation against the lead in your notes.
- If multiple agents bring their buyers, get cards / contact info. They become the "buyer agent outreach" list for the next price-drop refresh.
- Watch the time. If turnout is slow, post the second Stories frame around the midpoint.

## T-plus-0 (doors close)

- Lock up. Sellers' instructions take priority over any "could you also..." asks.
- Lockbox closed, lights down to seller's preference, pet released if relevant, signs collected.
- Post the wrap Stories frame.

## T-plus-2h

- Within 2 hours, log the open to the workspace:
  - Append to `nyoa-workspace/listings/<slug>/showings.md`: date, type=open-house, count, agent count, standout observation.
  - Add a follow-up task to `tasks.md`: "Send post-OH follow-up to each sign-in by EOD tomorrow."
- For each sign-in, kick off `/nyoa-buyer-seller-comms` with `post-showing-thankyou.md` if they engaged in any real conversation. Skip auto-following the drive-bys.

## Compliance check

- The agent's behavior at the open is the most visible part of the package. Don't ask attendees' demographic profile, religion, family size, or where their kids would go to school.
- If an attendee leads with a protected-class question ("is this a good neighborhood for [group]?"), redirect to neutral facts (commute, walkability, amenities). Don't characterize the neighborhood demographically.
- Don't volunteer school quality opinions. Districts are public record; rankings are not.
