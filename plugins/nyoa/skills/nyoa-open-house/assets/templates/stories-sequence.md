# Open House — Instagram Stories Sequence

**Required:** date, window, address short, one feature for the live frame, one moment for the wrap.
**Length:** three frames. Each frame is 1-2 short lines of overlay text.

---

## Frames

### Frame 1 — T-minus-4h (morning of)

Post around 9am the day of (for a 1pm open). Sets the day in motion for anyone scrolling.

```
{{date_short}}, open at {{address_short}}.
Doors open in {{hours_to_go}}h.
{{one_low_friction_detail — e.g., "Coffee will be made."}}
```

**Example:**
> Sunday, open at 312 Maple. Doors open in 4h. Coffee will be made.

---

### Frame 2 — Live (during)

Post within the first 15 minutes of the open being underway. Real, not staged.

```
{{number_or_pattern}} {{walk_through_observation}}.
{{address_short}}, until {{end_time}}.
```

**Example:**
> Five through in the first fifteen. Porch is doing the work today. 312 Maple, until 3.

---

### Frame 3 — Wrap (after)

Post within an hour of the open ending. Acknowledges turnout, calls out one moment, leaves a soft door open for the buyer who didn't make it.

```
{{turnout_one_word_or_count}}. {{moment_worth_remembering}}.
{{soft_cta_for_missed_buyers}}.
```

**Example:**
> Twenty-six through. The conversation by the screened porch was the one I'll remember. DM if you missed it — I'll book a private walk this week.

---

## Compliance check

- No demographic targeting in any frame.
- No "must see" / "won't last".
- Live frame is real activity, not a fabricated count.
- Wrap frame's DM offer must be genuine — the agent will actually respond.
