# Open House — Sign-In Card Copy

**Required:** agent first name.
**Length:** one short paragraph. Printed on the sign-in clipboard or shown next to a tablet.

The job: set expectations so attendees know how the info will be used. The agent will get more honest sign-ins by saying it plainly than by leaving it ambiguous.

---

## Skeleton

```
Thanks for stopping by {{address_short}}.

What I'll do with your info: {{honest_one_sentence — usually "one follow-up text or email, then quiet unless you ask"}}.

What I won't do: {{honest_one_sentence — usually "add you to a recurring marketing list"}}.

If a quick post-walk question would help, leave a number. Otherwise, just the email.

— {{agent_first}}
```

## Example

> Thanks for stopping by 312 Maple.
>
> What I'll do with your info: one follow-up text or email asking what you thought of the house. That's it.
>
> What I won't do: add you to a recurring marketing list.
>
> If a quick post-walk question would help, leave a number. Otherwise just the email.
>
> — Ann-Riley

## Compliance check

- No demographic targeting in the language.
- Whatever the agent commits to here must match what they actually do. Don't promise "one follow-up" then drop them into a 12-week drip.
- If the brokerage has a required sign-in template, use that and append this language.
