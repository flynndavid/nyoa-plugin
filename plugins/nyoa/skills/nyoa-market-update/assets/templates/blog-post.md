# Market Update — Blog Post

**Length:** 400-500 words.
**Required:** all stats, source, market name, month.

---

## Structure

1. **Title** — ≤ 60 chars. Specific to month + market. "April 2026 East Nashville Market Update" beats "Real Estate Market Update".
2. **Meta description** — ≤ 155 chars. One sentence with the headline number and the thesis.
3. **H1** — matches or paraphrases the title.
4. **H2 #1 — The number that matters** — lead with the single stat that frames the month. Plain language. No "in today's market".
5. **H2 #2 — What this means if you're buying** — concrete takeaway in 2-3 sentences.
6. **H2 #3 — What this means if you're selling** — concrete takeaway in 2-3 sentences.
7. **H2 #4 — Looking back at last month's read** *(optional — only include if a prior month's piece exists in the workspace)* — one paragraph on whether last month's thesis held up.
8. **Source citation** — at the end, before the soft CTA. "Numbers summarized from [source], current through [month]."
9. **Soft CTA** — one sentence inviting a conversation, not pushing a transaction.
10. **Compliance footer** (verbatim).

```
{{2-3_sentences_anchored_to_one_stat}}.

{{2-3_sentences_concrete_takeaway}}.

```

## Compliance check

- Every stat cites the source.
- No forecasts. "If the trend holds" is OK; "prices will go up" is not.
- No "great schools" / "diverse" / "safe" / "family-friendly".
- No "balanced market" without defining it.
- No "now is the time to" sales nudge.
- Thesis is the same as the IG and email versions — don't drift across channels.
