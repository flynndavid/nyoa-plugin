# Market Update — Instagram Caption

**Length:** ≤ 200 words + 12-15 hashtags.

---

## Structure

1. **Hook (line 1)** — the headline number, stated like a person, not a chart. "$542K median in East Nashville last month. Up 3.8% from a year ago." beats "April Market Update".
2. **One-line translation** — what the number actually means.
3. **The thesis** — same single sentence as the blog and email versions.
4. **One concrete takeaway** — for buyers OR sellers. Pick one. Two doesn't fit at this length.
5. **Source line** — single sentence: "Source: {{source_name}}, {{month_year}}."
6. **CTA** — one short line. "DM me for the full breakdown" or "Full post linked in bio".
7. **Hashtag block** — 12-15 tags. Skip the broad ones (#realestate, #realtor). Lean neighborhood + market + niche.

## Skeleton

```
{{headline_number_as_a_sentence}}.

{{one_line_translation}}.

{{thesis_one_sentence}}.

{{one_concrete_takeaway_for_buyers_or_sellers}}.

Source: {{source_name}}, {{month_year}}.

{{cta_one_line}}.

{{hashtags}}
```

## Compliance check

- Source cited.
- No forecast.
- No "great schools" / demographic claims.
- Hashtags don't include any banned/shadowbanned tags (agent verifies in-app).
- Thesis matches the blog + email versions.
