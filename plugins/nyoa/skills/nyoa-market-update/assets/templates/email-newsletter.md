# Market Update — Email Newsletter

**Length:** 300-400 words.

---

## Structure

1. **Subject** — specific number-driven. "19 days. The new normal in East Nashville." beats "May Market Update".
2. **Preview text** — ≤ 90 chars. One sentence pulling the thesis forward.
3. **Opening line** — what a person would actually say. Not "I hope this email finds you well".
4. **Numbers paragraph** — the 2-3 stats that matter, each translated. Not the full grid; not a chart.
5. **The thesis paragraph** — 2-3 sentences. Same thesis as blog and IG.
6. **Buyer takeaway** — one paragraph, concrete.
7. **Seller takeaway** — one paragraph, concrete.
8. **Link to the full blog post** — "Full breakdown is on the blog: [link]".
9. **Signature block** — agent name, brokerage, license, phone, email.
10. **Compliance footer** (verbatim).
11. **Unsubscribe** — required (CAN-SPAM).

## Skeleton

```
Subject: {{subject_with_a_number}}
Preview: {{thesis_compressed_to_one_line}}

Hey {{first_name}},

{{opening_line_a_person_would_say}}.

{{numbers_paragraph_2_or_3_stats_translated}}.

{{thesis_paragraph}}.

If you're buying right now: {{buyer_concrete_takeaway}}.

If you're selling right now: {{seller_concrete_takeaway}}.

Full breakdown on the blog: {{blog_link}}.

{{agent_first}}
{{agent_signature_block}}

All numbers in this post are pulled from {{source_name}} for {{period}}. Real-time reporting may differ — check the source before quoting any figure. Nothing here is a price prediction; the past is description, not promise.

{{unsubscribe_link}}
```

## Compliance check

- Source cited.
- No forecast.
- No demographic claims.
- License + brokerage in signature.
- Unsubscribe link present.
- Thesis matches the blog + IG versions.
