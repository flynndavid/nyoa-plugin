# Review Request Email

**Subject:** ≤55 chars, personal, references the transaction
Examples:
- "A quick favor after [property address]"
- "Would you mind sharing your experience?"

**Body:** ≤150 words. Structure:

```
[First name] —

[1 sentence referencing a specific memory from their transaction — not generic "thanks for your business." Example: "Watching you hand the keys to your daughter at closing was one of my favorite moments this year."]

If your experience working together was worth sharing, I'd really appreciate a quick Google review. It takes about 2 minutes and helps other [buyers/sellers] find the kind of help they need.

[Direct link to Google review / Zillow review — agent provides]

No pressure at all. Either way, I'm glad we got this done.

[Agent first name]
```

**Rules:**
- Reference a specific, real memory from the transaction (ask the agent if needed)
- One clear ask: leave a review
- Direct link (not "go to Google and search for me")
- No incentives ("leave a review and I'll...")
- No guilt ("it would really mean the world to me...")
- Short. Respect their time.
