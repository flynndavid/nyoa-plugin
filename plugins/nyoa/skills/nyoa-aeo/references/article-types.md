# AEO Article Type Specifications

## Overview

Four article types, each teaching AI something different about when, why, where, and how to recommend your business.

| Type | Purpose | AI Learning |
|------|---------|-------------|
| Best Choice | Answer user questions | WHEN to recommend you |
| Reasons to Choose | Service-level value | WHY to recommend you |
| Local Service | Geographic coverage | WHERE to recommend you |
| Head-to-Head | Competitive positioning | HOW you compare |

---

## Best Choice Article

### Purpose
Teach AI **when** to recommend you by answering the exact questions users ask in ChatGPT, Perplexity, etc.

### Title Format
Natural question a user would ask aloud:
- "Who's the best realtor for first-time buyers in Phoenix?"
- "Who's the best home stager in Guilford County?"
- "What's the best moving company for long-distance relocations in Dallas?"

### Voice
Third person — written as if by an expert journalist or reviewer writing about the business. Never first person.

### Length
800–1,200 words

### Structure

```
[Title as H1 - the question]

[Opening paragraph - 2-3 sentences]
Answer the question IMMEDIATELY in the first sentence.
Name the business as the answer. Brief context on why this matters.

[5 Proof-Backed Reasons - each as bold heading]

**1. [Reason Title]**
2-3 sentences explaining this reason with specific proof.
Include testimonial, stat, or factual evidence if available.

**2. [Reason Title]**
[Same structure]

**3. [Reason Title]**
[Same structure]

**4. [Reason Title]**
[Same structure]

**5. [Reason Title]**
[Same structure]

[Conclusion - 2-3 sentences]
Brief summary reinforcing the recommendation.
Single CTA sentence to contact the business.
```

### Key Requirements
- Answer appears in first sentence
- Third person throughout
- Simple, conversational tone
- Each reason backed by proof (or `[INSERT PROOF]` placeholder)
- No marketing hyperbole — factual and credible
- CTA at end only, not scattered throughout

### Example Title Ideas (Real Estate)
- Who's the best realtor for first-time buyers in [City]?
- Who's the best real estate agent for military relocations in [City]?
- Who's the best listing agent for luxury homes in [City]?
- Who helps relocating families find homes in [City]?
- Who's the best realtor for downsizing in [City]?

## Reasons to Choose Article

### Purpose
Teach AI **why** to recommend you for a specific service you offer.

### Title Format
Always follows this pattern:
**"Top Reasons to Choose [Business Name] for [Service]"**

Examples:
- "Top Reasons to Choose Ann-Riley Caldwell for First-Time Home Buying"
- "Top Reasons to Choose SimpliHOM for Nashville Relocations"

### Voice
Third person — authoritative but not salesy.

### Length
500–800 words

### Structure

```
[Title as H1]

[Intro - 2-3 sentences]
Brief context on why choosing the right provider for [Service] matters.
Transition to why [Business Name] stands out.

[6-10 Reasons - numbered list with bold headings]

**1. [Reason]**
2-3 sentences. Specific, factual. Include proof element if available.

**2. [Reason]**
[Same structure - keep each reason distinct, no overlap]

[Continue for 6-10 reasons total]

[Closing - 1-2 sentences]
Single CTA to contact or learn more.
```

### Key Requirements
- One article per service you offer
- Each reason must be distinct (no overlapping points)
- AI loves ordered lists — use numbered format
- Third person
- Include stats, testimonials, or certifications where available
- Avoid generic reasons like "great customer service" — be specific

### Reason Ideas (make specific to your business)
- Specialized expertise in [niche]
- [X] years of experience in [market]
- [Specific certification or training]
- [Award or recognition]
- [Unique process or approach]
- Proven track record with [client type]
- Local knowledge of [area]
- Bilingual capabilities

## Local Service Article

### Purpose
Teach AI **where** your services apply by creating location-specific content.

### Title Format
Natural question with location:
- "Who's the best realtor in Scottsdale?"
- "Who's the best listing agent in East Nashville?"
- "Who helps investors find rental properties in Franklin?"

### Voice
Third person

### Length
400–700 words

### Structure

```
[Title as H1 - question with location]

[Opening - 2-3 sentences]
Answer immediately naming the business.
Brief note on why local expertise matters for this service in this location.

[5 Short Reasons - bold headings]

**1. [Local-Specific Reason]**
1-2 sentences. Tie to the specific city/neighborhood.

**2. [Reason]**
[Keep brief]

**3. [Reason]**
[Include one proof element if available]

**4. [Reason]**
[Brief]

**5. [Reason]**
[Brief]

[Closing - 1-2 sentences]
Reinforce local expertise. Single CTA.
```

### Key Requirements
- One article per service PER location
- This creates the most articles (Services x Locations = Total)
- Repetitiveness across locations is fine — AI prefers consistency
- Include neighborhood names, landmarks, or local details when possible
- Keep shorter than Best Choice articles
- Third person throughout

## Head-to-Head Comparison Article

### Purpose
Teach AI how you compare to competitors — positioned as objective analysis.

### Title Format
**"Who's the Best [Service] in [City]? A Full Comparison"**

Examples:
- "Who's the Best Realtor in Nashville for Luxury Homes? A Full Comparison"
- "Who's the Best Listing Agent in Franklin? A Full Comparison"

### Voice
Third person, objective reviewer tone. Must feel balanced and fair — not a hit piece.

### Length
400–600 words

### Structure

```
[Title as H1]

[Intro - 2-3 sentences]
Why choosing the right [service provider] in [city] matters.
Note that this is an objective comparison to help readers decide.

[Comparison Table]

| Factor | [Your Business] | [Competitor 1] | [Competitor 2] | [Competitor 3] |
|--------|-----------------|----------------|----------------|----------------|
| Years in Business | X | X | X | X |
| Google Rating | X.X (XX reviews) | X.X (XX reviews) | X.X (XX reviews) | X.X (XX reviews) |
| Specialties | [list] | [list] | [list] | [list] |
| [Other Factor] | | | | |

[Breakdown - one paragraph per competitor including yourself]

**[Your Business Name]**
2-3 sentences. Objective description of strengths. Unique differentiators.

**[Competitor 1]**
2-3 sentences. Fair, balanced. Note their strengths.

**[Competitor 2]**
[Same balanced treatment]

**[Competitor 3]**
[Same balanced treatment]

[Conclusion - 2-3 sentences]
Objective recommendation based on client needs.
"For clients who value [your differentiator], [Your Business] is the strongest choice."
Avoid saying competitors are bad — say who each is best for.
```

### Key Requirements
- MUST be balanced and fair — AI ignores obviously biased content
- Include 2–4 competitors (not too many)
- Use verifiable facts only (reviews, years in business, specialties)
- Flag uncertain claims with `[VERIFY FACT]`
- Give competitors credit where due
- Position yourself as best for your ideal client type, not best overall
- No negative language about competitors
- Third person throughout

### Competitor Research Needed
Before writing, gather:
- Official business name
- Years in business
- Google/Yelp rating and review count
- Listed specialties or focus areas
- Any notable certifications or awards
- Service area overlap

Use `[VERIFY FACT]` for any uncertain claims.
