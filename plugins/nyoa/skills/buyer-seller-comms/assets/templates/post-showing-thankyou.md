# Post-Showing Thank-You

Sent within 4 hours of the showing. Goal: keep the buyer warm without pestering.

**Required inputs:** Buyer first name, address(es) shown, what they liked, what they pushed back on, next step.

**Channels:** SMS by default (fast). Email if you owe them a longer follow-up with comparable listings or info.

---

## SMS variant

```
{{first_name}} — appreciate you walking {{address(es)}} today. Heard you on {{their_specific_pushback}}. {{Next_step_in_one_sentence}}. — {{agent_first}}
```

**Example (one house, mild pushback):**
> Mike — appreciate you walking 123 Maple today. Heard you on the no-fence thing in the back; I'll get a fence quote from the seller this week and circle back. — AR

**Example (multiple houses):**
> Mike — appreciate you walking the three this morning. Sounds like Maple was the standout and Locust was a hard no. I'll send three more like Maple by Friday. — AR

---

## Email variant (when there's more to follow up on)

```
Subject: Quick recap from today's tour

Hey {{first_name}},

Appreciate the tour today. Quick recap of where my head's at:

What worked:
· {{address}} — {{their_specific_positive}}

What didn't:
· {{address}} — {{their_specific_negative}}
· {{address}} — {{their_specific_negative}}

What I'm doing next:
· {{specific_action_1}}
· {{specific_action_2}}

I'll have {{follow_up_thing}} for you by {{day}}. If anything else came up after we wrapped, hit me with it.

— {{agent_first}}
```

## Hard rules

- Reference one specific thing they said (proof you listened).
- Always end on a concrete next step the agent owns. No "let me know if you have any questions."
- No "thanks for your time" template-speak. "Appreciate you walking ___" is more specific.
