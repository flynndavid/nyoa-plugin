# Referral Ask (Post-Close)

Sent 2–6 weeks after closing. The window matters: too soon and the client is still mid-move; too late and the warmth fades.

**Required inputs:** Past client first name, property they bought/sold, close date, one specific moment from the transaction worth referencing, optional photo / detail of their post-move life if known.

**Channels:** Email (this is the keeper). Some agents pair with a hand-written card; the email reinforces.

---

## Structure

### Subject
Personal. Specific. No "Referral request" or "Quick favor".

Examples:
- "Quick check-in — how's the new kitchen treating you?"
- "Two months in at Maple — how's it living up?"

### Opening (1–2 sentences)
A specific reference to something from their transaction or their new life. Proof you remember them.

### The middle (3–4 sentences)
Honest reflection on the transaction + a specific way you'd love to be useful to people they know who are about to buy or sell. Skip the "I'm never too busy for your referrals" cliché.

### The close
One soft, specific ask + the path. Not "Send anyone my way!" — instead, a real prompt.

---

## Example

```
Subject: Two months in at Maple — how's it living up?

Hey Mike,

Saw on Instagram you finally got the bookshelves built into the alcove off the dining room — looked exactly the way we talked about during the walk-through. Glad it's coming together.

Reflecting on it: the back-and-forth with the inspection on Maple was easily the trickiest stretch I've had this year, and the way you handled the two days where we thought the deal was dead was a big part of why it didn't die. So — thank you, again.

If anyone in your circle is starting to look this fall — friends from the new neighborhood, colleagues, anyone you trust enough to send my way — I'd be grateful for the intro. The buyers I do my best work for are usually people coming via someone like you, who's already vouched for me. Even just a heads-up that someone's "starting to think about it" is helpful — I never push.

And separately: if anything in the house has come up that I can help track down (the original owner's contact, contractor recs, etc.), holler.

— {{agent_first}}
```

## Hard rules

- Reference one specific thing from their actual transaction or post-move life. No "Hope all is well!"
- The ask should be specific (a friend who's "starting to think about it") not vague ("anyone you know").
- Pair with a separate offer to be useful (rec, contact, info) so the email isn't 100% transactional.
- One ask. Don't bundle a "hey also can you write a Zillow review" — that's a different email.
