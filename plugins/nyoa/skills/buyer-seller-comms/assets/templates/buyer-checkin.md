# Buyer Check-In — "Still Looking?"

For buyers who went quiet 3–8 weeks after the last interaction. Goal: re-open the conversation without pestering.

**Required inputs:** Buyer first name, last interaction (date + what it was), criteria they were searching, current market signal worth flagging.

**Channels:** Email by default. SMS if they're a high-urgency buyer who's just gone quiet for a week.

---

## Email variant

```
Subject: Quick check on the {{neighborhood}} search

Hey {{first_name}},

Haven't bugged you since {{last_interaction_date}}. Couple of things on my radar that made me think of you:

· {{market_signal_1 — e.g., "Inventory in your price band ticked up 12% this week"}}
· {{market_signal_2 — e.g., "Two of the houses on your saved list dropped price"}}
· {{market_signal_3 — optional, e.g., "Rates moved a quarter point since we last talked"}}

Three buckets on where you might be:

1. **Still actively looking** — happy to set up another tour weekend.
2. **Pausing for a season** — totally fine, I'll keep watching and only ping if something matches.
3. **Closed the chapter** — also totally fine, just tell me and I'll stop emailing.

Which one are you in?

— {{agent_first}}
```

---

## SMS variant (short check)

```
{{first_name}} — quick check: still looking in {{neighborhood}}, or pausing? Either's fine, just want to know whether to keep sending you stuff. — {{agent_first}}
```

## Why this works

The 3-bucket close gives the buyer permission to say "no, I'm out" without ghosting, and gives them an easy "yes, still in" without committing to a tour. Either response re-opens the conversation cleanly.
