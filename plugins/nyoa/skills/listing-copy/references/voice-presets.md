# Voice Presets

When the agent has no per-agent voice file, choose a preset based on the property's price tier and character. Each preset adjusts vocabulary, sentence rhythm, what features to lead with, and what the buyer self-ID looks like.

If unsure, default to **starter** under $500K, **luxury** at $1.5M+, **NYOA house** in between. Fixer / investor / land are character-driven, not price-driven.

---

## NYOA house (default)

Warm, specific, confident. Concrete features over abstract adjectives. Sentence-length variety. Trust the buyer's intelligence.

- Lead with: a single concrete, image-forward feature
- Tone: friendly knowledgeable neighbor, not announcer
- Avoid: clichés, exclamation points (zero), "you'll love", "imagine yourself"
- Sentence rhythm: short → medium → short. Varied.

**Hook example:** "Quartz counters and a six-burner Wolf range anchor a kitchen built for someone who actually cooks."

---

## Luxury

Restrained, dimensional, specific. The reader is sophisticated; do not oversell. Name materials, designers, vintages, provenance.

- Lead with: provenance, material, or named feature (architect, designer, year, brand)
- Tone: gallery-card calm
- Avoid: "luxury", "exclusive", "premier", "stunning", "world-class", "estate" unless literal
- Sentence rhythm: longer, declarative. Confidence through specificity, not adjectives.
- Numbers in words (twelve-foot ceilings, not 12').

**Hook example:** "Original white oak floors throughout the main level, twelve-foot ceilings, and a kitchen redone in 2022 in soapstone, white oak, and brushed brass by Studio Carlin."

---

## Starter

Plainspoken, friendly, practical. The buyer is figuring out what they can actually afford and live with.

- Lead with: a feature that solves a real first-home concern (move-in ready, low maintenance, walkable, dog-friendly fenced yard, single-level)
- Tone: helpful agent over coffee
- Avoid: "starter home" (says nothing), "perfect for first-time buyers" (Fair Housing-adjacent — keep it about the house, not the buyer category)
- Sentence rhythm: short, clear, direct.

**Hook example:** "A renovated kitchen, a fenced yard big enough for a dog, and no work to do before move-in day."

---

## Investor

Numerical, math-forward. Lead with the financial case.

- Lead with: cap rate, cash flow, rent comp, condition
- Tone: spreadsheet collaborator
- Include: estimated rent if known, current condition, near-term capex
- Avoid: lifestyle copy, fluff, scenes
- Sentence rhythm: terse, fact-list style is fine

**Hook example:** "Off-market 4-unit in 12 South. $4,800/mo current rent, market rents trending $5,800. New roof 2023; HVAC and electrical original."

---

## Fixer / project

Honest about the work. Reward buyers who can see through to the finished house.

- Lead with: bones, original detail, structural soundness, what's salvageable
- Tone: candid, almost a renovation pitch
- Disclose: known issues you'd renovate around
- Avoid: "needs TLC" (cliché and ADA-adjacent), "great bones" (cliché), pretending it's move-in
- Sentence rhythm: practical, sequential

**Hook example:** "Original 1908 layout, walls of plaster and lath, hardwoods under the carpet on the first floor. Roof was redone in 2018; everything else is yours to take."

---

## Land

Spatial, sensory, oriented to the lot's defining geographic feature(s).

- Lead with: acreage + the single most distinctive land feature (creek, ridge, treeline, road frontage, view)
- Tone: a guide walking the property line
- Include: zoning, road access, utilities at the lot, topography
- Avoid: "buildable lot" without confirming, "cleared" without confirming
- Sentence rhythm: longer, place-based

**Hook example:** "12 wooded acres with 600 feet of creek frontage and a ridge that opens to a south-facing pasture at the back of the parcel."

---

## Fair Housing red-flag list (apply to every preset)

These phrases tank a listing regardless of voice. Strip from agent input and never write them yourself:

- "Great for families" / "perfect for kids" / "family neighborhood" / "growing family"
- "Walking distance to church / synagogue / mosque" — replace with "walking distance to [main street name]"
- "Christian / Jewish / Muslim community" or any ethnic / religious / national-origin neighborhood claim
- "Bachelor pad" / "perfect for newlyweds" / "couples retreat"
- "Master bedroom" → "primary bedroom"
- "Exclusive" when paired with anything implying protected class
- "Great schools" without a sourceable claim — replace with "in [district name] district" only if true
- "Quiet [ethnic] neighborhood"
- "Handicap accessible" → "single-level" / "step-free entry" / "wide doorways" / specific ADA features
