# Instagram Playbook

Six Instagram-specific modes for `/nyoa-social-content`. The base skill produces a multi-platform weekly mix; these modes go deeper on the IG-specific patterns most agents need.

Each mode gets its own section: when to use it, required inputs, structure, and a compliance note.

---

## Mode 1: `monday-planner` — full IG week

**When:** Sunday night or Monday morning. The agent wants the week's IG plan in one shot rather than figuring out each day separately.

**Required inputs:**
- Active listings or upcoming launches this week
- Showings / open houses scheduled
- Local events or news worth riffing on
- Behind-the-scenes moments the agent's open to sharing
- Anything to NOT post about (deals under wraps, family stuff, etc.)

**Structure:**

1. **Five feed posts** — one per pillar: Market education, Listing or sold, Behind the scenes, Community / neighborhood spotlight, Personal or lifestyle. For each: hook, full caption, 10-15 hashtags, suggested posting time, one-sentence visual direction.
2. **One Reel concept** — 15-45 second concept tied to one of the week's actual events. Hook (0-3s), three beats, CTA.
3. **Two Stories prompts** — one question-sticker prompt for replies, one poll for engagement.

**Compliance note:** every output gets the standard NYOA Fair Housing pass — no demographic targeting, "primary bedroom" not "master", no school quality claims, no clichés.

**Example skeleton:**

```
## Week of {{date_range}}

### Monday — Market education
Hook: "{{specific_number_or_pattern}}"
Caption: {{full_caption}}
Hashtags: {{10-15 tags}}
Posting time: {{suggested_time}}
Visual: {{one_sentence_visual_direction}}

### Tuesday — Listing or sold
...

### Reel concept (post {{day}})
Tied to: {{specific_event}}
Hook: {{on-screen text and voice-over}}
Beat 1, 2, 3
CTA: {{one_action_for_the_viewer}}

### Stories
1. Question sticker: "{{question_designed_for_replies}}"
2. Poll: "{{option_A}} vs. {{option_B}}"
```

## Mode 2: `welcome-dms` — new-follower greetings

**When:** Once a week, when the agent has gained more than ~10 followers since the last batch.

**Required inputs:** the new-follower list, with any context the agent has (location, what they engaged with, mutuals, whether they're a fellow agent or a buyer or a seller).

**Structure:** 5 distinct DM variations, each labeled with the angle it's playing:

1. **Friendly nudge** — casual, low-pressure, opens the door
2. **Local connection** — references the neighborhood or market explicitly
3. **Question opener** — asks one short question
4. **Soft hook** — names the agent's perspective without selling
5. **Quiet professional** — short, lets the follower decide what to do

**Rules:**
- Each DM ≤ 280 characters.
- No NYOA-banned clichés ("stunning", "must see", "nestled", "boasts", "luxury living awaits") and no DM-specific template phrasing ("just touching base", "hope this finds you well", "circling back").
- No demographic profiling of the follower based on their handle / photo.
- The agent rotates these across the new-follower list — same DM to everyone reads like automation.

**Compliance note:** Instagram DMs are messages, not marketing copy in the regulatory sense, but Fair Housing still applies — don't categorize the recipient by protected class.

## Mode 3: `info-replies` — handling "info please" on a listing post

**When:** Within an hour of a listing post collecting "info / send info / more details" comments. Algorithm rewards fast replies.

**Required inputs:**
- Listing details: address, price, beds/baths/sqft, 3-5 standout features
- Listing detail link

**Structure:**

A. **Public comment replies (3 variations)** — under 60 characters each. Brief, on-brand, no price or full address dropped publicly. Examples:
- "Sliding in your DMs."
- "Sending now."
- "Sent — check messages."

(These are very short on purpose. Variation keeps the post from looking automated; the actual details live in the DM.)

B. **DM script** — clean, scannable, ≤ 200 words:
- Quick thank-you for reaching out
- Four most relevant property details (bullet list)
- Listing detail link
- One open-ended qualifying question ("are you actively looking right now, or just keeping an eye on the market?")

**Rules:**
- Never name-drop the price or full address in the public comment. That's why the comment is short.
- The DM is where details live.
- The qualifying question is open, not yes/no — "what's your timeline?" beats "are you pre-approved?".

**Compliance note:** all the same Fair Housing rules. Don't characterize the property or the neighborhood demographically in the DM.

## Mode 4: `engagement-reset` — diagnostic + 7-day plan

**When:** Reach has dropped 30%+ for two consecutive weeks, or quarterly as a routine algorithm check-up.

**Required inputs:**
- Last 30 days of post performance (post type, topic, date, reach, likes, comments, saves, shares — full numbers if available, relative performance if not)
- Posting frequency this month
- Anything different the agent has been doing lately

**Structure:**

1. **Diagnosis (3-4 paragraphs)** — what the data says about WHY engagement is down. Be specific. If a content-mix problem (too many listing-only posts), say so. If a posting-cadence problem (too many in too few days), say so. If the data points to "it's not the algorithm, it's the content", say that too.

2. **Stop doing (3-5 bullets)** — concrete things to pause based on the data.

3. **7-day reset plan** — Day 1 through Day 7 with specific post type and topic for each day. Mix of formats (Reel, carousel, single image, Stories takeover).

4. **Engagement moves (5 bullets)** — what to engage with outside of posting (commenting on local agents, replying to comments within 30 min, sharing local-business content to Stories, DMing past clients with a market note, engaging in industry conversations on Threads / LinkedIn).

**Compliance note:** the diagnosis can call out content problems honestly without violating anything. Be direct — "your reach is fine, your content is repetitive" is honest feedback.

## Mode 5: `hashtag-audit` — refresh the hashtag set

**When:** Quarterly. Or whenever a hashtag block seems to be killing reach.

**Required inputs:**
- Current hashtag set (paste exactly as the agent uses it)
- Market name
- Niche or specialty
- Approximate follower count and average engagement

**Structure:**

1. **Audit table** — for each current hashtag, label it (branded / location / niche / broad / oversized / undersized / stale-or-risky) and recommend keep or drop with a one-line reason.

2. **Refreshed set (25-30 tags)** — structured:
   - 3-5 branded (agent's own / brokerage's signature tags)
   - 5-8 location (city, neighborhood, market)
   - 8-10 niche (luxury / first-time / investment / specialty)
   - 4-6 broad
   - 0 oversized, 0 banned

3. **Approximate post count per recommended hashtag** — so the agent can verify the size category in-app.

**Rules:**
- Don't recommend a tag we can't verify isn't banned. If unsure, flag for the agent to check in Instagram.
- Don't fabricate post-count numbers. Round to nearest order of magnitude ("~80K", "~250K"). Note that counts change over time and the agent should re-verify quarterly.
- Suggest two or three rotation sets rather than one — Instagram flags accounts using the identical hashtag block on every post.

**Compliance note:** hashtags themselves are short enough to slip Fair Housing violations through. Audit `#familyfriendly[neighborhood]`, `#diverse[neighborhood]`, etc. — drop them.

## Mode 6: `just-sold` — four matched outputs from one input

**When:** Within 24 hours of closing, with the client's permission to post.

**Required inputs:**
- Address (or neighborhood-level if the client wants privacy)
- Sold price (or "above asking" / "at asking")
- List price
- Days on market
- Side (listing / buyer / both)
- The story — one memorable detail about the transaction

**Structure:**

A. **Instagram caption** — ≤ 200 words + 12-15 hashtags. Leads with the story, not the address.

B. **Instagram Stories sequence** — 3 frames:
- Hook (sold-sign image + one-line headline)
- Story (one-sentence on the moment that mattered)
- Gratitude + CTA (thank the client by first name or initials, open the door for DMs)

C. **Reel script** — 15-30 seconds:
- Hook (0-3s)
- Three beats (3-25s)
- CTA (25-30s)

D. **Facebook post** — longer than IG, community-focused, includes one question that invites comment, agent + brokerage + license number footer required.

**Rules:**
- Client permission to post is required and assumed; if the agent hasn't checked, the skill asks before drafting.
- "Above asking" is OK; never publish the exact sold price unless the client said yes.
- The story has to be true. Don't dramatize.
- The Facebook variant always carries the license-and-brokerage footer.

**Compliance note:** "just sold" is one of the highest-violation content categories because agents commonly describe the buyer demographically. Strip "perfect family", "young couple", "growing family". Describe the property or the moment, not the people who bought it.

---

## How the modes get invoked

The agent can invoke a mode in one of two ways:

1. **Slash-command argument** — `/nyoa-social-content monday-planner`, `/nyoa-social-content welcome-dms`, etc. The skill recognizes the keyword and routes to that mode.
2. **Natural language** — "Plan my IG week" / "Welcome my new followers" / "Process the info comments on my listing post". The skill matches the trigger phrases and routes to the right mode.

When the mode is ambiguous, the skill asks. Don't guess.
